# Claude's PvZ journal

## Current lessons

1. Never plant sun producers (sunflower or sun_shroom) in a row that zombies walk down, especially cols 2-4; this run lost 4 sun_shrooms and 2 sunflowers that way and the economy never recovered.
2. Place sun producers only in the back columns (0-1) or behind a wall_nut, and replace them immediately if they die, since night income comes only from them.
3. Every row zombies enter needs a sustained damage source: buy a peashooter early for each lane that gets zombies, because rows with 0 attackers let zombies walk straight to the house.
4. Coneheads (~370 armor) and screen doors take far more damage than a lone wall_nut can absorb; when one appears, add a second wall or a fume_shroom behind the wall before it reaches col 3.
5. Fume_shroom works best directly behind a wall_nut so zombies bunch in its short radius, and the wall must be replanted the moment it breaks.
6. Cherry_bomb was in the loadout but never bought; save 150 sun for when two or more zombies bunch in one row near cols 2-4, since it clears a clump at once.
7. Keep at least 50 sun in reserve for an emergency wall_nut and count income before every purchase so you never reach wave 9 with under 60 sun.
8. Lawn mowers are emergency tools: this run spent four by wave 7 on single zombies, leaving none for waves 8-9, so use a mower only when a row is about to reach the house with no answer.
9. Stagger damage across rows so two armored zombies do not stack in one row; a single unanswered row breaks through first.
10. Avoid planting on graves (cells marked grave block placement) and plan lanes around them before wave 1.
11. Do not spend early sun on potato mines or extra sun_shrooms in the lane; zombies reach them before they arm and the sun is gone for the rest of the game.
