# Claude's PvZ journal

## General lessons

1. Never let the queue stall: if sun is unspent, queue the next cheap defensive plant right away instead of waiting on one expensive item.
2. Put the first attacker in each land row before extra producers; the first zombies arrive by wave 2.
3. Don't queue duplicate or already-occupied cells; a rejected or cancelled item wastes time.
4. Check the attacker count in every row each wave; a row with 0 attackers is lost, so rebuild shooters immediately.
5. Queue the cheapest working defender first and don't reorder the queue; replacing queued items cancelled plants.
6. Build sunflowers and sun_shrooms early, but stop at 4-5 producers and spend the rest on defense.
7. Put a fume and a wall_nut in every row before wave 3; lone early zombies kill col 0 producers.
8. Verify the board against the written plan each wave; the plan was again mostly not executed.
9. A fired mower is gone; put 2 more shooters and a wall in that row at once.
10. Keep a 150+ sun reserve going into waves 8-10 so you can rebuild when a mower fires.
11. Build wall_nuts in cols 4-5 of every row by wave 4 and check they are on the board.
12. Use cherry_bomb on conehead clumps before they reach the shooters.
13. Replace damaged shooters before the wave instead of after they die.
14. Don't rely on one-shot plants (squash, potato_mine) as main defense.
15. Pole vaulters and dolphin riders jump the first plant, so keep a second wall behind it.
16. Skip puff_shroom; spend on fumes and walls.

## Night levels

1. Night: start with sun_shrooms in cols 0-1 in the rows, then a few sunflowers. There is no falling sun, so income only grows while you plant producers. Stop around wave 4-5.
2. Night: the winning build was fumes in cols 1-3 or 1-4 (2-3 per row), puff_shrooms in cols 3-4, and nuts in cols 5-6 in every row. This held with all 5 mowers kept.
3. Night: put the first fume_shroom in each row in col 2 or 3 before wave 3, then add a second. Fumes go through screen doors. Wave 5 is the first real test, so have a nut and a second fume in every row.
4. Night: the pressure points are waves 8-10 and wave 20. Row 0 got hit hardest on wave 20 (lost fumes in cols 1-2 and a nut), so give row 0 an extra nut and a spare fume before the final wave.
5. Night: in the final wave, keep sun (300+) for instant replants of fumes and nuts and fire cherry_bomb at the first big clump. Replant lost fumes at once, since fumes with nuts in front kept killing.
6. Night: don't plant potato mines in cols 0 or 7 during an active wave. They get eaten before arming. Plant them in col 4 during quiet gaps.
7. Night: about 175-200 sun per minute from about 4-5 sun_shrooms plus a few sunflowers was enough to sustain the defense. Don't overbuild producers beyond that.
8. Night: use mowers only as a last resort and rebuild a row's defense right after a mower fires. All 5 stayed this time.

## Night conveyor levels

1. On conveyor levels, use each card as it arrives so the belt never fills. A full belt stops new cards, and cards are free, so spending them costs nothing.
2. Hold doom_shroom and ice_shroom for football and dancer clumps (waves 10+). Place doom at cols 4-6 where zombies bunch up, because the crater blocks that cell for later planting. Ice buys time for the fumes to kill.
3. Use hypno_shroom on footballs and other big threats in cols 3-5. The hypnotized zombie fights for you and absorbs the push.
4. The belt's cheap shooters (scaredy_shroom, puff_shroom, fume_shroom) died often, roughly 16 lost in total. Replace fumes in the row first, because scaredy_shrooms are weak blockers and die to footballs.
5. Row 1 and row 2 lost their mowers (waves 14 and 20), and rows 0-2 leaked the most. Give the open rows extra fumes and keep a hypno or doom in reserve for them.
6. Use grave_buster on graves in cols 4-5 only when nothing urgent needs the card. Free cells there give more room for fumes in front of the line.
7. Don't waste doom_shroom on a lone zombie (e.g. waves 17-19 single-zombie rows). Save it for rows with footballs or screen doors, and use ice or a mower for the rest.

## Pool levels

1. Dolphin riders jump the first plant in water, so put a pad+tall_nut at col 6 as bait with shooters at cols 2-4 behind it.
2. Keep 3 pad+shooters per water row, preferably snow_pea, by wave 8.
3. Land rows 0, 1 and 5 have no mower and broke at waves 19-30 to zomboni; put 2 walls in front and keep a cherry_bomb for each zomboni.
4. Zomboni crushes walls and shooters in a land row; cherry_bomb it near col 5-6 as it arrives, or snow_pea it to slow it.
5. Rebuild a dead pad and its shooter together on the same turn.
6. Keep a cherry_bomb ready from wave 10 for dolphin riders and clumps; it recharges very slowly, so use it as soon as it is ready.
7. Skip potato mines here; spend the sun on snow_pea and walls.
8. Plant extra sunflowers early, since pads add 25 to every water plant, and replace dead sunflowers because late waves kill them.

7. Wave 30 swarmed row 3, so keep jalapeno and spare pads ready for the last wave.

## Fog levels

2. Don't put sun_shrooms or sunflowers in col 0 of a row with no shooter; put producers behind a shooter.
3. Sun income was far too low (only 1 sun_shroom and 1 sunflower got built); plant 2 sunflowers first, before any mushroom.
4. Rows 2 and 3 are water: lily_pad then shooter in cols 1-3 by wave 4.
5. Bring cherry_bomb, snow_pea or puff-free cheap options; 100-sun peashooters starved the economy.
6. Place potato_mines only behind a wall_nut or a fume; lone mines get eaten before arming.
7. Fog hides zombies, so keep shooters in cols 1-3 of all 6 rows rather than reacting.
8. Save cherry_bomb for wave 8-10 conehead clumps in rows whose mower is gone.
