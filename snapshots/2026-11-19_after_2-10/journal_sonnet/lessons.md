# Claude's PvZ journal

## General lessons

1. Fume_shrooms are the main killers. Put 2-3 per row in cols 1-3 and replace dead ones at once, including rows 0 and 4. This level 6+ fumes died, mostly to footballs.
2. Footballs are the most dangerous zombie: they killed fumes in rows 1, 3 and 0-2 in waves 13, 15 and 19-20. Save hypno_shroom, ice_shroom and doom_shroom for them and don't spend them on plain zombies or coneheads.
3. Use one-use cards as soon as a clump (football, screen_door, dancers) reaches cols 4-6, before it touches the fume line. Doom in col 4-6 hits the whole clump and ice buys time, but doom leaves a crater that blocks that cell for good.
4. Build the economy first (sun_shrooms or sunflowers in cols 0-1) and stop adding producers around wave 4-5. Don't put producers in a row with no fume_shroom, because a leak kills them.
5. Put a wall_nut in cols 4-5 in front of the fumes and replace it below 50%. Check every row each wave while the lawn is empty, because once the mowers are spent one leak ends the level.
6. Puff_shrooms are free, so use them as col-3 and col-4 fillers. They die to coneheads and screen doors, so replant them every wave.
7. Potato_mine needs 15 s to arm and gets eaten if planted late. Plant it 15-20 s before the zombies arrive, at col 4, away from an active fight.
8. Pole vaulters jump the first plant. Back up each wall_nut with a second fume_shroom or an armed potato_mine.
9. Keep 150+ sun in reserve for waves 8-10 and the final wave so I can replant at once. Fire cherry_bomb on clumps before col 3 instead of holding it.
10. Graves block cols 5-8 in some rows, so plan the defense in cols 0-5. Use grave_buster only when I have spare sun or a free card.
11. The final wave and waves 8-10 are the biggest, with coneheads, flags, screen doors and dancers together. Rebuild dead fumes and nuts before them.

## Night levels

1. Night: start with sun_shrooms in cols 0-1, then a few sunflowers. There is no falling sun, so income stops growing once you stop planting producers (stop at wave 4-5).
2. Night: this winning build was col 0 sun_shrooms in rows 1-4, 2 fumes in cols 1-2 in every row, puffs in col 3-4, nuts in cols 5-6. Rows with 3+ attackers held, and row 0 was thinnest (no sun_shroom, only fumes) and took the worst damage in wave 20.
3. Night: the final wave is a flood of coneheads, flags, screen doors and dancers in every row at once, and it burned all 5 mowers. Before wave 20, have full-health nuts in every row, a third fume per row, and 2 cherry_bombs ready if possible.
4. Night: puff_shrooms in col 3 died to coneheads in waves 5-8 (4 losses). They still buy time, but replant them each wave since they cost 0 sun.
5. Night: with 375 sun unspent at the end, I under-spent. Convert surplus sun into extra fumes and fresh nuts in rows 0-1 by wave 15, since they were damaged (nut 2%, 23%) and ended weakest.
6. Night: the dancing zombie with backup dancers and conehead clumps are the late threat. Bomb them in cols 4-5 before they reach the fumes, not in col 7 once they are mixed with a crowd.
7. Night: replace a fume or nut at once when it dies in a row, in the lull between waves, rather than during the final rush when replants get eaten (potato_mine eaten 3 s after planting).
8. Night: don't count on mowers. Treat any fired or lost mower as a failed row and rebuild its defense right away with a fume plus a nut.

## Night conveyor levels

1. Conveyor night level: the belt gives only free single-use cards (grave_buster, doom_shroom, ice_shroom, hypno_shroom, scaredy_shroom, fume_shroom, puff_shroom). There is no sun economy, so the plan to build sun_shrooms was irrelevant here. Use each card on arrival and don't let the belt fill.
2. Don't waste grave_buster cards early. Use them only when the belt is near full and nothing better is available, since they take a few seconds and graves in cols 6-8 don't matter.
3. Hold doom_shroom and ice_shroom for waves with footballs, screen doors and dancers (waves 10+). They are very slow to recharge, so keep one in hand for the final wave instead of using every copy early.
4. Plant fume_shrooms whenever they arrive and fill every row evenly. Rows 0 and 4 and row 2 ended with only 1-3 attackers, and the weakest row broke first. Scaredy_shrooms in cols 0-2 died constantly, so treat them as filler rather than a defense.
5. Put hypno_shroom on the front football or screen_door in a row, so it turns the strongest zombie into a blocker. Place it in cols 4-5 in front of the fumes, not behind them.
6. All five mowers were gone by wave 20, so the last waves have no safety net. Keep at least one doom or ice for the final wave and use the mowers' help only as a last resort.
