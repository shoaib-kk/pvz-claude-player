# Claude's PvZ journal

## General lessons

1. Give every row the same defense: at least 3 shooters plus a wall_nut in front; the weakest row is the one that breaks, so recount attackers per row every wave.
2. Build the economy first (4-5 sunflowers in cols 0-1, and replace any that die), then stop at wave 4-5 and spend everything on defense.
3. Rows 0 and 5 break early to single conehead leaks (wave 5-9); put 3 shooters and a wall_nut in them before wave 5, not a lone peashooter.
4. Rebuild every lost shooter, pad, sunflower, or wall_nut on the next turn, especially in a row whose mower is gone; a row with 0 attackers is already lost.
5. A fired mower is gone for good; once one fires, double that row's shooters and walls immediately and bomb the zombie clump.
6. Upgrade to repeaters/snow_peas by wave 8-10 in rows that have lost plants; plain peashooters can't kill buckethead (~70 peas) fast enough.
7. Place wall_nuts in cols 4-5 of every row early and replace any under 40%; zombies were reaching col 2-3 shooters because nothing stopped them.
8. Don't let sun sit near zero late: at wave 18 I had 25 sun and nothing affordable, so keep income up and bank 150+ for emergencies from wave 15.
9. Keep cherry_bomb ready for buckethead/conehead clumps before col 3 and use it on the row that is about to break, not on single zombies.
10. Replace damaged plants before the last waves instead of counting on them.
11. Don't let a lone zombie wander through a row with no shooters; put at least one fresh shooter plus a wall in that row right away.

## Night levels

1. Night: start with sun_shrooms in cols 0-1 in the rows, then a few sunflowers. There is no falling sun, so income only grows while you plant producers. Stop around wave 4-5.
2. Night: the winning build was fumes in cols 1-3 or 1-4 (2-3 per row), puff_shrooms in cols 3-4, and nuts in cols 5-6 in every row. This held with all 5 mowers kept.
3. Night: put the first fume_shroom in each row in col 2 or 3 before wave 3, then add a second. Fumes go through screen doors. Wave 5 is the first real test, so have a nut and a second fume in every row.
4. Night: the pressure points are waves 8-10 and wave 20. Row 0 got hit hardest on wave 20 (lost fumes in cols 1-2 and a nut), so give row 0 an extra nut and a spare fume before the final wave.
5. Night: in the final wave, keep sun (300+) for instant replants of fumes and nuts and fire cherry_bomb at the first big clump. Replant lost fumes at once, since fumes with nuts in front kept killing.
6. Night: don't plant potato mines in cols 0 or 7 during an active wave. They get eaten before arming. Plant them in col 4 during quiet gaps.
7. Night: about 175-200 sun per minute from about 4-5 sun_shrooms plus a few sunflowers was enough to sustain the defense. Don't overbuild producers beyond that.
8. Night: use mowers only as a last resort and rebuild a row's defense right after a mower fires. All 5 stayed this time.

## Night conveyor levels

1. On conveyor levels, use each card as it arrives so the belt never fills. A full belt stops new cards, and cards are free, so spending them costs nothing.
2. Hold doom_shroom and ice_shroom for football and dancer clumps (waves 10+). Place doom at cols 4-6 where zombies bunch up, because the crater blocks that cell for later planting. Ice buys time for the fumes to kill.
3. Use hypno_shroom on footballs and other big threats in cols 3-5. The hypnotized zombie fights for you and absorbs the push.
4. The belt's cheap shooters (scaredy_shroom, puff_shroom, fume_shroom) died often, roughly 16 lost in total. Replace fumes in the row first, because scaredy_shrooms are weak blockers and die to footballs.
5. Row 1 and row 2 lost their mowers (waves 14 and 20), and rows 0-2 leaked the most. Give the open rows extra fumes and keep a hypno or doom in reserve for them.
6. Use grave_buster on graves in cols 4-5 only when nothing urgent needs the card. Free cells there give more room for fumes in front of the line.
7. Don't waste doom_shroom on a lone zombie (e.g. waves 17-19 single-zombie rows). Save it for rows with footballs or screen doors, and use ice or a mower for the rest.

## Pool levels

1. Water rows need a lily_pad under every plant, so budget 25 extra per plant early; when a pad dies its plant dies too, so rebuild both together.
2. Build both water rows to the same standard as land rows: pad+peashooter in cols 2-3 at least, then upgrade to repeater/snow_pea, with a pad+wall_nut in col 4-5.
3. Row 3 ended with 0 attackers and bare water; keep at least 3 live pad+shooters in each water row at all times.
4. Row 2 had only 2 pad+peashooters at the end; add a third pad+shooter and a pad+wall_nut, not bare pads.
5. Aim cherry_bomb at a water-row or land-row clump chewing at cols 2-4 (it can go on a pad or open water); do it before the shooters die.
6. Land rows 0 and 1 of the pool level lost most plants to conehead/buckethead groups in waves 9-16; give them snow_peas and walls by wave 8.
7. Keep 150+ sun in reserve at waves 15-20 for emergency rebuilds, since losses show first in rows 0, 1 and 3.
8. Potato_mines only go on land rows cols 4-5, early and shielded.
