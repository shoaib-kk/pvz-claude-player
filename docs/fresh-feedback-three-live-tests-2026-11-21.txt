What still went wrong
====================
1. The player repeatedly bought extra pots at 25 sun while waiting to afford
   sustained attackers, and sometimes delayed its first attacker substantially.
2. It continued proposing attackers on the street side of zombies that had
   already passed them. In attempt 3, decision 150 put cabbage at (2,7) with the
   lead buckethead already near col 6; decision 151 recognized the limitation.
3. Some targets lost their pots during 4-18 seconds of model thinking. Median
   decision times were 5.33s, 5.41s and 5.30s; maxima 13.87s, 18.07s and 14.27s.
4. Emergency resources were poorly timed. Attempt 2 selected Cherry Bomb but
   never planted one. Attempt 3 planted one at wave 19, after substantial lane
   damage. Attempt 3 also selected Jalapeno but never planted it. Both
   final-wave losses broke row 0 with no live attacker.
5. The player made explicit cost-reading errors despite correct input:
   decision 36 called a pot "0 net after collection" and decision 123 called
   it "0-sun". Both snapshots list pot cost 25 and the arithmetic line is
   "flower_pot: 25-25=0 sun". The zero is the remaining balance, not its price.
   Spelling out "cost 25; balance after purchase 0" is a candidate future
   presentation fix, not something changed during these tests.
6. Objective labels sometimes overstate what their counters measure. For
   example, "Pot the pole-vault lane" specified only a pot count, no row;
   its met status establishes that count, not protection of the actual lane.

Assessment
==========
Feedback freshness and accounting worked in the live run, but the player still
failed all three attempts. Two final-wave losses are promising observations,
not evidence of a reliable win-rate improvement. The next useful check is a
bounded reasoning/presentation comparison on recorded mistakes (cost versus
remaining balance, forward direction, and emergency timing), preserving the
same board and journal context. No further model calls or live attempts were
made after the requested third result.
