Decision interface and accounting changes - 21 November 2026

Implemented
===========
- Moved placement rules into placement.py; the executor and legal-cell report
  use the same validator. It enumerates unranked legal single-step targets,
  without adding supports, choosing plants, or claiming firing reach.
- Identical legal-cell masks share one list. Funding and recharge remain in the
  seed table, rather than being repeated for each list.
- Plant actions require exact packet names and integer coordinates. The JSON
  schema distinguishes plant, shovel and break_vase; model decisions constrain
  names to the relevant packets where the loadout is fixed. Local queue edits
  reject malformed shapes before altering a valid pending queue.
- Added observation timestamps and clarified that legal cells can change during
  a player call. Existing live placement validation remains authoritative.
- Added a purchase ledger: confirmed packet spending, initial plants supplied
  at takeover, unattributed later plants, replacement value of missing plants,
  and spending on purchased plants later missing are separate measurements.
  One-use disappearance and intentional shovel/upgrade removal are not counted
  as destroyed investment. Shovel layers use the actual confirmation snapshot.
- Selection/planning now show the furthest recorded same-level result and the
  latest two attempts, including loadouts and completed planting counts. Unknown
  historical starting-cleaner counts are labeled unknown, not assumed equal.
- The player can choose up to three measurable planting objectives, optionally
  specifying coordinates and a wave deadline. Subsequent boards show confirmed
  completions, currently present counts and deadline status. Completion timing
  uses the confirmation snapshot. Objectives never schedule or choose moves.
- Reflection receives tagged accounting and objective evidence. Historical
  sun_lost fields are presented as replacement value, not cash spending. No
  existing player lessons or historical run records were rewritten.

Limits
======
Plant provenance follows observed cell/type lifetimes and confirmed actions;
unseen replacements are not decoded from hidden game fields. The ledger marks
unknown provenance/cost rather than inventing purchases. A disappeared plant's
damage contribution and removal cause remain unknown without further evidence.
No strategy, layout or automatic emergency choice was introduced. The changes
have offline coverage and one final player replay; no new live-game validation
has been performed for this implementation.
